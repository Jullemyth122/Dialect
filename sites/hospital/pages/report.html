Questions to ask your doctor

  • What do these results mean for me?
  • Are any of these results outside the lab's range, and does that matter in my case?
  • Do I need to repeat any test, or prepare differently next time (for example, by fasting)?
  • How do these compare with my earlier results?
  • Is there anything I should do, or stop doing, because of these results?

Test explanations: Source: MedlinePlus, National Library of Medicine. Dialect sends only each test's LOINC code to MedlinePlus to fetch them.
