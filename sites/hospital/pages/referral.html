Referred patient: results review

The other hospital's results use its own codes and units. Dialect lines them up with {{name}}'s, converted to your units with cited factors only.

When do I use this?

A patient treated at another hospital is referred to you. Their earlier lab results use that hospital's test names and units: blood sugar in mmol/L, for example, while you use mg/dL.

Instead of converting by hand, you see both visits on one table in your units. You don't approve anything here; each hospital's lab already matched its own codes.

How to read the table

  • One row per test, earlier visit on the left.
  • converted means changed to your units; the original value is underneath.
  • Red box: tests nobody could match. Don't compare them.
