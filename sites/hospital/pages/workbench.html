Lab code mapping

Teach other systems what your lab's test names mean. Do it once; every result after that is translated automatically.

Why and when do I do this?

Your lab names tests its own way, like GLUF or CREAT. Other hospitals, health records and public-health systems don't know those names. They understand LOINC, one worldwide code per lab test.

You map your tests when your hospital starts sending results to a partner, joins a health-information exchange, changes lab systems, or adds a new test.
